### Inferences

- A compact gallery could use all 13, or use 1–10 and 12–13 if only one clearly gender-neutral seated portrait is wanted.
- Attribution can be placed in a caption or a dedicated image-credits block, provided it remains reasonably associated with each photograph.
- Commons is the original licensing reference here; the images should not be relabeled as Pexels/Unsplash-license or public-domain assets.

### Gaps

- The Commons pages do not provide model-release documentation. This does not negate the copyright licenses, but a publisher should assess personality/privacy rights for its particular editorial use and jurisdiction.
- Candidate 2's page offers more than one license; these notes deliberately select CC BY-SA 3.0 rather than attempting to list every option.

### Inferences

- Best opening gallery sequence: 5, 8, 12, 10, 1, 3. This alternates fashion, texture, color, dance, product and care/damage imagery.
- Candidate 4 is better for a care/storage section than for illustrating individual construction details because the garments overlap.
- Candidate 6 should remain documentary and matter-of-fact; avoid suggestive cropping or sensational headlines.

### Gaps

- Visual review establishes relevance and non-explicit presentation, but it cannot independently verify garment fiber, brand, denier or a model's gender beyond the linked source descriptions.
